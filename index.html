About

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at NC State, specializing in AI and machine learning, and a researcher in Prof. Qiao Jin's Extended Horizon Lab.

I build AI-powered XR and AR systems with Unity, eye tracking, computer vision and multimodal sensing. What I keep coming back to is systems that notice what a person is doing and respond before being asked.
