I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at North Carolina State University (Class of 2027), specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I work as an undergraduate researcher in the Extended Horizon Lab, advised by Prof. Qiao Jin, building AI-powered XR and AR systems with Unity, eye tracking, computer vision, and multimodal sensing.

My interest is in systems that notice what a person is doing and respond before being asked — treating gaze and physiological signals as an input channel rather than a measurement recorded after the fact.
