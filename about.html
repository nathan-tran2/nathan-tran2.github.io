Background

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at North Carolina State University, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. What I keep coming back to is systems that notice what a person is doing and respond before being asked, treating gaze and physiological signals as an input rather than a measurement recorded after the fact.

That shows up across the work: eye tracking to tell an AI agent when to speak up, biosignals to decide when a coaching cue will land, stereo vision to make a projected play space respond to the people in it. Before the lab I spent nearly three years teaching computer science and web development at Pine Lake Preparatory.

I'm certified through the CITI Program for human subjects research, which means I can run studies with participants rather than only build the systems they use.
