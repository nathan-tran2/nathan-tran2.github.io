PulseCoach: Cardiac-Timed XR Stress Regulation Coaching with State-Aware AI

Read the paper (PDF)

Overview

Training for high-pressure moments such as medical handoffs, crisis negotiation and de-escalation checks what a trainee says and does, not whether their nervous system can hold up. PulseCoach reads that state directly and coaches the trainee through it.

How it works

  1. Read the bodyAn EmotiBit wearable streams EDA, HRV, per-beat PPG and IMU signals over Lab Streaming Layer.
  2. Hold the pressure until it’s safeThe scenario only escalates once the body has actually recovered.
  3. Choose the cueA state-aware AI coach picks a breathing cue, a redirect or a pause from the live biometrics.
  4. Time it to the heartbeatEach cue appears in XREAL glasses in the diastolic window of the heartbeat, predicted 300–500 ms ahead from detected R-peaks, when the body is most receptive to it.
