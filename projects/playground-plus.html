Overview

A projection-based mixed-reality play system that pairs a home projector and webcam over a shared floor space.

What I did

  • Integrated a ZED 2i stereo camera while keeping webcam support, taking real-time performance from roughly 15 FPS at 480p to 60 FPS at 720p.
  • Added depth sensing and perception on top of it, so the AI agent could gauge where people were in the space and how they were moving.
  • Extended the capture pipeline from a single fixed camera to a multi-camera, multi-angle rig that sees the play space from several viewpoints at once.
  • Planned the full physical deployment: camera placement, projector selection, mounting, interaction space, computing requirements and budget.
