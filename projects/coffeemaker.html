Overview

A recipe and inventory service built twice, once solo and once with a team, as the foundation for the course's full-stack work. A Spring Boot REST backend handles recipes, ingredients and inventory, and a React frontend consumes it over Axios.

The interesting part

Making a recipe walks its ingredients against current stock, refuses the whole order if any single one is short, and only then deducts every amount and saves. A half-made drink can never leave the inventory inconsistent.

How it's built

  • Both builds use a layered controller, service, repository structure with DTOs and hand-written mappers at the boundary.
  • The team version made ingredients a first-class entity with its own repository and endpoints, so new ones can be added at runtime instead of being baked into the schema.
  • Roughly 130 JUnit tests across the two builds, covering repositories, services and controllers.
