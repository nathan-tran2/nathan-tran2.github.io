MRPilot: Supervising and Intervening LLM-Based Multi-Robot Teams through Mixed Reality

Read the preprint

Overview

Large language models let a person direct a team of different robots in plain language, but they also make it hard to see how an instruction was interpreted or to change it once work is underway.

Grounded in a formative study with 12 non-expert users, MRPilot treats supervision as four stages and represents both the plan and its progress as structured commitments, shown across linked situated and overview views.

  • Forming a plan
  • Reviewing it
  • Following execution
  • Repairing it

Results

In a within-subjects study with 20 participants in a simulated virtual home, MRPilot lowered workload and improved situational awareness, transparency, trust and perceived control, compared with an LLM chat baseline running the same planner and the same robot capabilities.
